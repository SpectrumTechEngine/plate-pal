    Who is this for?

    A first name or nickname, shown on the summary. Kept on this phone only.

    🚫 Allergies and intolerances

    Tick only the ones a doctor has confirmed or that you know cause a problem.

    🧪 Additives you've noticed

    Things that seem to upset your child. There's no proof these affect everyone. This is just what you've noticed.

    👅 Tastes and textures

    Plate Pal looks for clue words in the name and ingredients, so treat these as a heads-up, not a sure thing.

    ✏️ My own words

    Any food, ingredient or brand to flag, e.g. tomato, mushroom, mint.

    💾 Backup

    Your lists live only on this phone. Save a backup now and then, or to move to a new phone. (The website and the Android app keep separate copies.)

    ⭐ Safe foods

    Foods your child trusts. Scan one to add it. If its ingredients change later, Plate Pal will tell you, because a recipe change can make a favourite food feel "wrong".
